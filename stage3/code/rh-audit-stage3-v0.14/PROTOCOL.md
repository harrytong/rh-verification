## First implementation: exact collection contract

GeometryContext.lean copies the successful Stage 2 baseline and renames its
theorem. Five trace contexts are inserted without altering its proof steps.
These expose local variables at the three selected predicate applications and
before the final consumer. The printed elaborated proof records inferred arguments.
GeometryContext.diff makes all changes reviewable. No arbitrary-P generalization
or dependency elimination is claimed by this copied proof.

HeightContext.lean spells out two checks: the newly constructed object's height
equals the assigned p.ht; and the constructed ExtraAt record satisfies its declared
hExpl6 equality. Success would establish only those equations in the imported
environment. It would not prove equality with a separately defined intrinsic
height on the original D.

The positive fixture covers predicate parameters, proof arguments, dependent
data and assigned height. The negative fixture must fail specifically with Lean's
type-mismatch diagnostic. These validate basic compilation/printing and failure
handling, not a future automated use classifier. There is no such classifier in
this release. Failed project probes are recorded as probe failures; classify
interface/type inference/resource issues before interpreting them mathematically.
