## Interpretation rules fixed before results

- Found argument: syntactic occurrence in the searched stored-expression projection, not semantic necessity or certified hypothesis discharge.
- Capped search: inconclusive, regardless of whether a hit was found before another branch limit.
- Product projection: extraction from an existing record, not evidence of constructing that record.
- Constructor application: a candidate construction site. Inspect its actual arguments and surrounding premises before claiming an unconditional witness.
- Function/lemma argument: a locally supplied obligation; check its instantiated type before claiming it discharges the intended mathematical premise.
- A field with type EMtTheta, or a `geo` field, does not establish satisfiability, non-vacuity, or faithful mathematical interpretation.
- True/empty/zero-looking inputs are candidates for review, not evidence of a flawed headline theorem by themselves.
- Statements about all uses, all callers, necessity, or full mathematical validity are out of scope.
